The plan accurately preserves the negative research-five conclusion and earlier uncertainty. Historical reported means/intervals agree with the saved reports. It does not call the unchanged population policy a winner or propose another unchanged confirmation. Qualified actual reward remains primary, clustered by seed over all six families; matching/cardinality, calibration and pause counts are diagnostics. Hidden actor/world truth and scenario labels remain audit-only; deployable model fitting and teachers use public observations and labels.

The proposed finite five-test reporting family uses alpha .01 / 99% intervals for any newly restated family claim, preserving original historical reports. At SD .26, the noncentral-t 90% power calculation and10% inflation give406 minimum / 447 fixed clusters for +.05. The selected policy variance rule is fixed before E and explicitly heuristic; no E-driven sample extension or unjustified power claim when the900-cluster ceiling binds. Prior four-testalpha is not mixed with the new rule as an unlimited lifetime guarantee.

All first-screen learning models are bounded to L1/L2; the other six-paradigm methods are a gated backlog. N1–N7 plus L1/L2 and G/P/K make12 distinct entries, not 12 new policies. The simple single-replicate arithmetic2160+720+2880+4320+1920+5364=17,364 matches the plan. Training/validation are conditional, and the maximum is not a commitment to run every cell. Three collectors of a shared seed remain correlated.

B admits at most two mechanisms; C requires positive baseline and own-parent effects with frozen ablations; D admits no candidate unless mean ≥ +.025 and one-sided 90% lower > 0. These are explicitly exploratory selection gates. They may stop with greedy and no E. A fresh fixed E still determines superiority; noisy D selection is not promoted as a proof of the best policy. The stricter90%gate versus the critic's80% proposal is a stated prospective decision rather than a post-result switch.

The deadline section correctly distinguishes inherited unconditional expected pause/reward proportionality from source-specific conditional/Rao–Blackwell action invariance. The family-dependent delayedconstant6039/17150 is supported by EXTENDED_THEORY.md's preassignment tower argument; no assumption that total pauses are independent of prior deadline marks is made. Actual reward stays primary.

Information restrictions are adequate at the planning level: full JSON-only observation inputs, exact hard legality after queries, permanent declines, missing-feature integration, no source PRNG reconstruction, no privileged teacher labels, no future disclosure leakage and no unsupported IPS/DR on deterministic matching histories. Oracle/null outcomes are not called global attainable bounds. Numerical LP/quadrature is labeled an uncertified diagnostic until envelopes and relaxation proof exist.

## Strongest remaining objection

The plan can be statistically honest and still spend substantial compute on a winner's curse. Forty-seed B has roughly .0366 SE for an old SD .231 contrast; searching many candidates can produce +.05 point gains under a null. C's positive-point contribution criteria do not themselves establish mechanism causality. D's lower-bound gate is a reasonable conservative heuristic, but selecting its best candidate can also favor noisy variance estimates. Fresh E protects the final claim, not wasted compute. Preserve the prior smallergridobjection, exactparent controls, at-most-two promotions and no-candidate termination rule. Do not replace a failed mechanism with a new tuned variant inside the same seeds.

Bounded rollout caps, posterior quadrature and source-prior query samplers are proposals, not measured production limits or calibrated prediction claims. Require public-likelihood/graph/fidelity tests, observed inference/disk costs and separate correctness review before any fresh reward stage. The expected occupancy LP needs its own proof under adaptive public histories; unconditional latent-conditional relaxation is not automatically a tight deployable ceiling. A privileged heuristic's null result is not proof of no headroom.

Full exactlogs may exceed 197 GiB; forecasting with25% headroom + 20 GiB reserve is a heuristic guard, not a storage guarantee. Operational interruption preserves partial attempts and every committed cell; no all-attempt success claim follows from completed rows. Securearchive restoration and final manifest closure are future obligations, not already completed round-six checks.
