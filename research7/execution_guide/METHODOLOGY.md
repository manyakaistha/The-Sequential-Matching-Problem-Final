## Method and priority order

For every candidate freeze its hypothesis, parent, implementation/config/assets, eligibility rules, trigger denominator, predicted action change, falsifier, resource cap and independent reviewer before collecting its evaluation grid. Classify evidence as observed artifact, source deduction, exact reduced model, approximation, untested hypothesis or unknown.

Prioritize Q1 and A1 to test cheap information/allocation mechanisms; audit whether H1 has exploitable repeat history; then investigate D1 as the lead heavy continuation mechanism. This is scheduling priority within the existing portfolio, not a new selection rule. Q2/D2 and learned variants remain conditional on their original dependencies. Empty slots stay empty.

| Mechanism | Required admission evidence | Reward control and falsifier |
|---|---|---|
| Q1: collision-aware hard queries | Known contradiction/decline handling, legal single 12-unit ask batch, complement/collision fixtures; unchanged G after answers | G and unchanged Q6; reject promotion if graph/query improvements do not survive the registered actual-reward gate |
| A1: whole static allocation | Independent tiny-graph oracle, odd cycles, cardinality/weight traps, deterministic quantization/ties and legal bounded fallback | P with identical scorer and asks; K diagnoses cardinality. Surrogate objective gain alone fails |
| H1: released response history | At least 200 actors with two mature trials, at least 5% feasible-edge opportunities informed beyond the prior; whole-world held-out response validation | Static P with the same asks/allocation, history disabled. Reject unusable signal in paused/exited actors or actors lacking unused partners |
| D1: joint continuation | Deployed controller/reduced-oracle agreement; separate qualification, pause, survival and return laws; no reward double counting; regret <=.01 on held-out constructed grid; <=10% switch reversals in registered sensitivity checks | A1 own parent; mean-return/no-wait controls when required for the intended claim. Unsupported regions and boundary overflow must use the declared fallback |
| Q2/D2: value-directed query packages | Correct joint answer/disclosure branches; hypothetical and deployed matching controller agree; D2 additionally needs D1 fidelity and surrogate MAE <=.01, ranking agreement >=90%, false switches <=5% | Q2 uses FIFO+A1 and Q6+A1; D2 uses FIFO+D1, Q6+D1 and immediate-value acquisition with the same D1 controller. Defer if required controls cannot fit |
| L1/L2/A2/X1 | Original count, validation, fidelity, asset and dependency gates; explicit finite fit allocation | Keep original parents and controls. Predictive accuracy, teacher agreement or speed alone cannot promote |

Actual matching always checks exact eligibility, availability, no repeated dyad and no overlapping actor. All asks precede answers in one batch. Inputs are public observations and declared public-trained assets only; no hidden world fields, future calendars or ID decoding. Feedback maturity and late feedback must follow simulator semantics. Day-59 assignments receive full eventual qualified reward through follow-up; future decision value ends at day59. Pending assignments may inform occupancy/history but must not be booked as new reward again.

Record successive mechanism steps separately: changed ask batch, changed refreshed feasible graph, changed complete matching, changed later assignment sequence, and qualified reward. Define numerator and eligible-opportunity denominator before collection. Tie changes or random action disagreements alone do not establish the proposed mechanism. Coupled models share actor-profile draws across incident edges and keep the same profile through answer revelation and downstream simulation. H1 response probabilities and P ranking scores are not calibrated qualified-eventual-reward probabilities. A1 may use explicitly labeled ranking weights; D1/Q2/D2 require admitted qualified-reward and separate occupancy models.

## Resource sizing and hours-long operation

For current Mac-only preparation, default two simulation workers total; one numerical thread per process. Agent slots are separate: root plus at most three children across the program. No agent starts its own simulator pool. At most three simulation workers are permitted globally, but a third Mac worker needs reviewed calibration. The current coordinator CLI supports at most two workers; a higher-worker implementation requires its own review.

Shared current Mac caps: 160 simulation/test CPU-hours, 24 fit CPU-hours, 100 GiB new output, 30 GiB free reserve, 10 GiB combined working RSS. The original portable 7a limits (6 CPU-hours, 8 GiB output, 4 GiB reserve, 2 GiB RSS) are historical host limits, not six additional fresh CPU-hours or a separate pool here. Register all prior consumption attributable to this program, tests, failed attempts, profiling, replays, archives and temporary overlap. Hardware capacity is measured at launch; lower actual limits prevail.

For each candidate/controller/recorder stratum measure conservative cost estimates: CPU seconds, stored bytes, elapsed seconds, peak RSS and observed tails. Freeze how the upper estimate is derived; a small-sample maximum is not a guaranteed bound. Include retry/replay and test overhead explicitly. With planned attempt counts a_p, require:

    CPU forecast = 1.5 * sum_p(a_p * upper_CPU_p) + reserved other work
    disk forecast = 1.5 * sum_p(a_p * upper_bytes_p) + assets/indexes/archive overlap

Both the upcoming batch and the remaining program must fit available registered budgets and free-space reserves. For a chunk of k complete clusters with m arms, nominal attempts are 6*m*k, plus its explicit verification/retry allowance. Choose k satisfying CPU, disk and desired wall envelope using measured process throughput; parallel wall time is not summed worker elapsed time divided by an assumed speedup. Fit jobs have a separate resource profile. Stop before admission if the forecast fails; a documented pre-data amendment may reduce scope or add registered storage, never erase existing evidence.

The reference smoke recorded about 5.983 CPU seconds and 11.96 MB output per attempt. Multiplying by the full planned ceiling gives about 165 CPU-hours and 1,107 GiB **before** candidate/retry overhead: a scale warning, not a forecast. The offline codec reduced two artifact categories by 93.592% on its selected sample; other artifacts and policy memory were excluded. Do not use that percentage as a whole-program storage guarantee.

During long jobs publish durable completed/expected cells, attempt states, leases, errors, cumulative CPU, disk/free reserve, RSS and ETA ranges. Check at chunk closure and stop immediately at live guards. During P6 show health only; no rewards, pooled/machine means, variances, confidence intervals or significance. Prepare a closed handoff after each chunk so an idle/new session can resume from registration and ledgers, without conversation memory.

## Statistical decisions and honest conclusions

For each policy/seed/family use Y=100*qualified introductions/arrivals by day59, zero if arrivals are zero. Coverage has that same arrival denominator and distinct served actors. Average six paired family differences within each seed; seeds are independent inferential clusters. Complete all registered policies/families, validate shared initial-world hashes and denominators, and do not drop failed cells. Replays do not create new samples.

Apply master-plan gates at full precision: P3/P4 require mean gain >=.025 versus G and their other registered mechanism/family/coverage conditions; P4 also requires positive own-parent gain. P5 requires its nominal one-sided 90% G bound >0 and other conditions, ranking by the minimum six-panel lower bound with the prescribed tie rules. These allocate/select work, not confirmation.

Before P6, independently compute n from P5 variances using sigma_j=max(.30,1.10*sd_j), the registered noncentral-t equation and 10% sample inflation. Freeze n, exact cells and candidate before any P6 world. If n>1,800 or budgets fail, do not launch the advertised powered study; any lower-powered design needs a reviewed pre-E amendment and honest limitations.

Confirmation uses the six simultaneous Bonferroni two-sided intervals at round family alpha .01. Adoption requires every lower bound >0, observed mean gain versus G >=.025, valid complete episodes and closed provenance/production/statistical reviews. Observed .025 does not certify a minimum .025 improvement; that needs a lower bound exceeding .025. Report all panel contrasts and family/service diagnostics. Nonsignificance does not imply equivalence or absence of harm.

First durable committed results are authoritative, including invalidity. A committed policy invalidity disqualifies that version. Infrastructure retries keep original cells/configurations and failed-attempt evidence; no best-attempt choice. Freeze health-only confirmation until exact grid closure and operational review; no significance stopping, alternative contender, code fix or outcome-driven sample extension. An unrecoverable incomplete study remains incomplete.

Independent verification reconstructs counts, action digests, denominators, seed differences and intervals from raw public evidence. Exact discrete agreement and STATISTICS.md's 1e-10 numeric tolerance govern final statistics, distinct from the earlier rounded smoke tolerance. Merge raw cells globally once; do not average machine means or combine machine p-values. Preserve null results, failures, objections and all attempts.
