import math
from scipy.stats import t, nct

sd_d = 0.267494019150511
sd_floor = 0.26
sd_mult = 1.1
sigma = max(sd_floor, sd_mult * sd_d)
delta = 0.05
alpha = 0.01
power_target = 0.90
sample_mult = 1.1
max_n = 900

print(f"Observed SD in D: {sd_d:.6f}")
print(f"Inflated sigma: {sigma:.6f}")

def calc_power(m):
    df = m - 1
    crit = t.ppf(1 - alpha/2, df)
    ncp = delta * math.sqrt(m) / sigma
    # Power for two-sided test: P(T >= crit | ncp) + P(T <= -crit | ncp)
    pow = nct.sf(crit, df, ncp) + nct.cdf(-crit, df, ncp)
    return pow

# Search for m
m = 10
while calc_power(m) < power_target:
    m += 1

n = math.ceil(sample_mult * m)
n_final = min(n, max_n)

print(f"Minimum clusters m: {m} (power: {calc_power(m):.4f})")
print(f"Inflated confirmation clusters n: ceil({sample_mult} * {m}) = {n}")
print(f"Final capped n: {n_final}")

## 3. Categorization of Scientific Knowledge

### I. Proven Mathematical Results
1. **Delay-Integrated Unconditional Expectation Identity**: Under independent draws across scenario families, $\mathbb{E}[Q \mid \text{family}] = c_f \mathbb{E}[P \mid \text{family}]$, where $c = 0.36$ in standard scenarios and $c = 6039 / 17150 \approx 0.3521$ in delayed-feedback scenarios.
2. **Non-Submodularity of Feasible Edge Revelation**: Pairwise hard constraint acquisition creates complementary information rather than submodular marginal returns; discovering attributes for actor $u$ strictly increases the marginal value of evaluating candidate partner $v$.
3. **Algebraic Connectivity in Disconnected Systems**: Global graph algebraic connectivity $\lambda_2 = 0$ whenever multiple disconnected components exist, regardless of internal matching augmentations.
4. **Irreversible Action Information Neutrality**: An irreversible decision eliminates subsequent information value if and only if no subsequent feasible decision is coupled to the shared state or regime.

### II. Exact Simulator Calculations
1. **Reward Function**: Primary score is Qualified MSMI per 100 arrived members (mutual acceptance, date $\le 30$ days, post-date feedback $\le 3$ days).
2. **Daily Ask Budget**: Exactly 12 ask budget units per day (3 per hard bundle, 1 per soft query).
3. **Sandbox Parameters**: 10-second timeout, 1 MiB maximum JSON request/response payload, unprivileged container execution (`network=none`, read-only root, UID 65534).
4. **Scenario Portfolio**: Six synthetic scenario families (`development`, `sparse`, `cold_start`, `delayed`, `shift`, `drift`), balanced with equal weight inside each seed cluster.

### III. Exact Simplified-Model Solutions
1. **Small-World Dynamic Planning**: On small constructed matching graphs (2 pairs, 4 actors), exact dynamic programming demonstrates that collision-aware joint constraint acquisition achieves the theoretical upper bound when feedback latency is negligible.
2. **2-Query Combinatorial Coordination**: Enumerating and ranking candidate complementary pairs over 28 dyads achieves exact collision-avoidance within a single day's budget.

### IV. Empirical Findings
1. **Stage B Screen ($n=40$ clusters)**: `N3` (+0.03333 MSMI/100) was the sole candidate passing the $\ge +0.025$ admission gate. Candidates `K` (+0.01875), `N1` (+0.00625), and `N2` (-0.02917) were rejected.
2. **Stage C Replication & Ablation ($n=60$ clusters)**:
   - `N3` replicated a positive point difference of +0.04167 MSMI/100.
   - `N3_fifo4` produced exactly **0.00000** difference vs `G` across all 360 episodes, proving that simply expanding query capacity to 4 bundles in FIFO order provides zero improvement.
   - `N3_two` achieved +0.04306 MSMI/100, matching full 4-bundle combinatorial evaluation (+0.04167, paired $p=0.9613$) with lower variance (SD 0.2481 vs 0.2646) and achieving a positive 90% one-sided lower bound (+0.00154).
3. **Stage D Independent Selection ($n=80$ clusters)**:
   - Both `N3` (+0.040625) and `N3_two` (+0.040625) cleared the effect threshold ($\ge +0.025$) and positive 90% lower bound.
   - `N3_two` achieved a higher lower bound (+0.001975 vs +0.001083) and lower paired SD (0.2675 vs 0.2737), winning selection under Occam's razor / lower tier complexity.
4. **Stage E Untouched Confirmation ($n=571$ clusters, 6,852 episodes)**:
   - Mean paired difference between `N3_two` and `G`: **+0.003211 MSMI/100** ($SE = 0.010715$).
   - Primary 99% CI ($\alpha = 0.01$): **[-0.024483, +0.030904]** ($p = 0.764559$).
   - **Scenario Family Decomposition**:
     - `cold_start`: **+0.034151**
     - `delayed`: **-0.034151**
     - `development`: **+0.012259**
     - `shift`: **+0.005254**
     - `drift`: **+0.004378**
     - `sparse`: **-0.002627**
   - **Mechanistic Root Cause of Attenuation**: The benefit in `cold_start` (+0.0342) is **exactly canceled** by the penalty in `delayed` (-0.0342). Unconditional query coordination front-loads queries without knowing whether feedback will arrive in time, incurring deadweight loss when responses are delayed. Across the balanced distribution, the net benefit vanishes.
5. **Production Verification**: 100.0% ordered, memory, and semantic action equivalence verified across 48 direct, 48 subprocess, and 12 restricted-container replays (12,960 total invocations) on Docker image `sequential-round6:smoke`.

### V. Approximation Assumptions
1. **Variance Planning Heuristic**: Sizing assumed $\sigma = 0.2942$ ($1.10\times$ inflation over Stage D). Observed confirmatory SD was $s_d = 0.2560$, validating the conservative safety margin of the planning design.
2. **Central Limit Asymptotic Normality**: Confirmed by 10,000-replicate whole-cluster bootstrap yielding near-identical intervals (99% CI: $[-0.023351, +0.031232]$).

### VI. Unvalidated Hypotheses
1. **Online Latency Regime Detection**: The hypothesis that an online Bayesian filter could infer whether an episode is operating under delayed vs instantaneous feedback and dynamically throttle upfront queries remains unvalidated.
2. **Stochastic Dual-Price Reservation**: The hypothesis that shadow prices from an LP relaxation could effectively hold scarce members for future arrivals without suffering deadline losses remains unvalidated in live evaluation.

## 4. Most Informative Unresolved Question

> **Can *adaptive latency-aware* querying policies—which modulate their query spending dynamically as a function of measured response latency—achieve positive net value without degrading performance on delayed-feedback regimes?**

Because `N3_two` achieved strong positive gains in `cold_start` (+0.0342) but suffered equal losses in `delayed` (-0.0342), the fundamental bottleneck of static query coordination is its inability to distinguish whether responses will mature before matches expire.
