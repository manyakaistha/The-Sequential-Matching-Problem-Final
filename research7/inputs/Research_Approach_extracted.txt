The challenge is a finite-horizon policy problem, not independent pair

ranking. On decision day t in {0,...,59}, the observable state is

state, undisclosed answers and unreleased outcomes are excluded [1].

Each day has two legal phases: submit one batch of clarification requests, receive results and refresh the observation,
then submit a feasible non-overlapping introduction batch. There is no intra-day adaptive question-by-question loop.
Objective and Ranking

For episode s, let Ms be qualifying introductions and Ns members arrived by day 59:
                                                        Rs = 100 × Ms / Ns
One qualifying pair counts once. The primary score averages episode scores within each of six families, then averages
the six family means equally. Tie-breaks are coverage, mutual acceptances per 100, lower ask cost and lower inference
time [1].

RESEARCH FOUNDATION


Established Tools, Challenge-Specific Integration
The proposal combines established ideas without claiming that their combination is already proven. Reciprocal
recommendation motivates modelling value to both endpoints [2,3]; active feature acquisition motivates paying for
information only when it can improve a downstream decision [5]; Edmonds supplies the general-graph matching
abstraction [4]; selective-label and delayed-learning work motivate time-safe, cautious updating [6,8].

conditional events:


                                    μ̂ = P̂(A | x ) × P̂(D | A, x ) × P̂(S | D, A, x )

A is recorded mutual acceptance; D is a qualifying date within 30 days of assignment; S is two timely 'Yes'
second-meeting intentions. This is a chain-rule factorisation of nested events - not an independence claim about the

PRIMARY CONTRIBUTION


Allocation-Aware Clarification
Let V*(Ot) be the estimated value of the best current feasible matching. For a legal question q with answer model P̂(a |
Ot, q):


                                       aVOI(q) = E [V*(O after q = a)] - V*(O )
                                                        a      t                                     t
                                                priority(q) = max{0, aVOI(q)} / c(q)


Uncertainty alone is insufficient. A question matters only if plausible answers can unlock feasible edges, alter edge
values, or change the coordinated allocation. Declined or undisclosed answers are explicit scenarios in the answer
model. This is an approximation to decision-relevant information value, not an exact POMDP solution [5].

Paired Uncertainty Analysis

Compute within-seed, within-family differences; report family means and dispersion. Where sample size supports it, use paired bootstrap
intervals over episode seeds while preserving family structure. Report raw counts when outcomes are sparse. A zero count in a small
episode is not evidence of equivalence.

  What Would Falsify or Weaken the Hypothesis
  The thesis is weakened if aVOI fails to beat baseline clarification, global matching does not beat greedy in paired rollouts,
  calibration does not improve decisions, gains vanish after coverage/runtime controls, or any assessed episode is invalid. It is falsified
  for this challenge if the full policy fails to outperform all three official baselines on the pre-specified family-averaged primary metric.
