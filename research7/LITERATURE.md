# Primary-source research and applicability

Primary abstracts/proceedings/documentation inspected during planning, 11 October 2026; finalized 12 October 2026. This is a targeted literature review across acquisition, general-graph matching, reusable-resource dynamics, waiting, decision-focused learning, selective labels and delayed feedback. It is not an exhaustive proof search or a claim that every paper's proof was reproduced. No literature source establishes a win in this simulator.

| Primary source | Useful idea for R7 | Transfer limit/test |
|---|---|---|
| Valancius, Lennon, Oliva, [Acquisition Conditioned Oracle for Nongreedy Active Feature Acquisition](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/valancius24a.html), ICML 2024 | Nongreedy, jointly informative acquisition; Q2/D2 use action value rather than entropy | Their oracle/generic-feature task is not this batch hard-revelation/occupation process; test T04/T06/controller consistency |
| Delong et al., [Online Bipartite Matching with Reusable Resources](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.07084) | Correlated selection and persistent resource-use decisions | One-sided bipartite deterministic usage differs from general-graph, two-actor stochastic busy + pause; no competitive-ratio import |
| Goyal, Iyengar, Udwani, [Online Allocation of Reusable Resources via Algorithms Guided by Fluid Approximations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.03983) | Occupancy laws and fluid relaxations motivate D1 continuation | Large-inventory/stochastic-duration assumptions differ from individual dyads and correlated reward/retirement; no full-sim upper bound inferred |
| Akbarpour, Li, Oveis Gharan, [Thickness and Information in Dynamic Matching Markets](https://web.stanford.edu/~mohamwad/DynamicMatching.pdf), JPE 2020 | Timing and departure information can interact with market thickness | Their patient policy sees critical departures unavailable here; waiting is not universally beneficial. Test known-vs-unknown-calendar toy and public arrival model |
| Golovin, Krause, [Adaptive Submodularity](https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.3967) | States conditions for greedy information guarantees | Hard two-endpoint revelation violates diminishing returns in T06; do not assert the theorem applies |
| Mandi et al., [Decision-Focused Learning: Through the Lens of Learning to Rank](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/mandi22a.html), ICML 2022 | Learn decision-relevant ordering; motivates bounded L2 teacher | Teacher/model value is not realized counterfactual reward; public features only, then policy rollout |
| Wei, [Decision-Making Under Selective Labels](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/wei21a.html), ICML 2021 | Label collection and future utility are linked | Matching actions/pause/censoring are coupled; their policy results don't identify unchosen-pair outcomes here |
| Zhou, Xu, Blanchet, [Learning in Generalized Linear Contextual Bandits with Stochastic Delays](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2019/hash/56cb94cb34617aeadff1e79b53f38354-Abstract.html), NeurIPS 2019 | Delayed-feedback-aware updates | Bandit contexts/actions are not coupled matching-resource transitions; regret bounds don't transfer |
| Jiang, Li, [Doubly Robust Off-policy Value Evaluation for Reinforcement Learning](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/jiang16.html), ICML 2016 | Sequential rather than row-wise OPE framing | Deterministic matching logs lack full action support; DR cannot create overlap; final inference uses direct rollouts |
| Bertsekas, [Rollout Algorithms and Approximate Dynamic Programming for Bayesian Optimization and Sequential Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.07998) | Lookahead from a fixed base controller and declared approximations | Approximation/sampling/model error prevent importing universal policy improvement; include parent/fallback and exact tests |
