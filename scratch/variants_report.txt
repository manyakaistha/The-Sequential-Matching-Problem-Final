1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The sequential reciprocal matching benchmark defines 6 distinct environment variants:
  1) 'development' : Baseline canonical environment with standard distributions.
  2) 'sparse'      : Severe geographic fragmentation (12 uniform zones, 0 cross-zone).
  3) 'cold_start'  : Low initial profile completeness (20% vs 35% complete, 70% missing).
  4) 'delayed'     : Elongated real-world feedback delays (date_day +5 to +12 days).
  5) 'shift'       : Preference weight regime shift (pace dominant, lifestyle sign flip).
  6) 'drift'       : Temporal non-stationarity / platform fatigue (-0.5 logit drift at day >= 35).

Across 60 decision days (+40 follow-up days) with 200 members:
- 'sparse' is by far the most challenging variant, reducing available feasible pairs by ~83%, slicing introductions from ~100 down to ~18, and crashing coverage to 14.5%.
- 'cold_start' creates a severe information bottleneck, exhausting ask budgets (cost 244-246 vs 198-200) and reducing match volume by ~35%.
- 'delayed' breaches the 30-day MSMI qualification deadline and locks members in "busy" state for up to 39 days.
- 'shift' inverts soft feature importance (lifestyle flips from +0.25 to -0.25, pace doubles to 0.8), severely punishing naive unweighted policies.
- 'drift' cuts post-day-35 mutual acceptance probabilities by 20% to 57%.


2. EXACT MATHEMATICAL SPECIFICATION & PARAMETERS (kit.py)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The synthetic generator `generate()` and simulator methods `_prob()` and `advance()`
differentiate variants via the following exact rules:

A. Geographic & Zone Parameters:
   - 'development', 'cold_start', 'delayed', 'shift', 'drift':
     * Zones sampled from 4 skewed zones:
       P(zone_a)=0.60, P(zone_b)=0.23, P(zone_c)=0.12, P(zone_d)=0.05
     * Cross-zone acceptance:
       With probability 0.30, truth['acceptable_zones'] includes a 2nd zone:
       dict.fromkeys([zone, r.choice(['zone_a','zone_b','zone_c','zone_d'])])
   - 'sparse':
     * Zones sampled uniformly from 12 zones:
       zone = r.choice(['zone_' + str(i) for i in range(12)])  (P = 1/12 = 8.33% each)
     * Cross-zone acceptance:
       STRICTLY EXCLUDED: truth['acceptable_zones'] = [zone] (100% single-zone only)

B. Profile Completeness & Missingness Parameters:
   - 'development', 'sparse', 'delayed', 'shift', 'drift':
     * P(complete profile upfront) = 0.35
     * If incomplete, each field observed independently with P = 0.10
     * Unobserved field status: 'declined' with P = 0.07, else 'not_asked' (0.93)
     * Initial missing hard constraints: ~58.7%
   - 'cold_start':
     * P(complete profile upfront) = 0.20  (43% reduction in complete profiles!)
     * Initial missing hard constraints: ~70.3%
     * Initial feasible pairs in raw pool: 16.6 (vs 33.4 in development, -50.3%)

C. Feedback Delay & Timing Parameters:
   - Introduction response: delay ~ Uniform{1..7} days. Deadline: day + 7.
   - If both accept:
     * Base date scheduling delay: max(response_days) + Uniform{1..14} days.
     * 'delayed' variant penalty:
       date_day += Uniform{5..12} days.
     * Consequence on MSMI qualification:
       MSMI requires: date_occurred_day - intro_assigned_day <= 30.
       In development: max delay is 7 + 14 = 21 days (100% within window).
       In delayed: max delay is 7 + 14 + 12 = 33 days. Dates occurring >30 days
       fail MSMI condition regardless of mutual interest!
     * Member lockout (busy state):
       Members locked until date_day + 6. In 'delayed', busy lock can last 39 days,
       severely diminishing pool liquidity.

D. Acceptance Probability & Soft Feature Weights (`_prob`):
   z = -0.25 + actor['bias'] + fit + shared + drift
   P(actor accept) = 1 / (1 + exp(-z))
   where fit = sum(w_k * (1.0 if f[k] == g[k] else -0.5) for k, w_k in weights.items())

   Weights:
   | Feature            | Standard / Dev / Sparse / Cold / Delayed | 'shift' Variant | Delta / Impact |
   |--------------------|------------------------------------------|-----------------|----------------|
   | relationship_goal  | +0.70                                    | +0.25           | -64.3%         |
   | relationship_pace  | +0.40                                    | +0.80           | +100.0% (2.0x) |
   | lifestyle          | +0.25                                    | -0.25           | Sign Inverted! |
   | conversations      | +0.20                                    | +0.50           | +150.0% (2.5x) |
   | emotional_avail.   | 0.00 (unweighted in first meeting)       | 0.00            | Unweighted     |
   | space_for_rel.     | 0.00 (unweighted in first meeting)       | 0.00            | Unweighted     |
   | relocate           | 0.00 (unweighted in first meeting)       | 0.00            | Unweighted     |

E. Temporal Drift Parameters:
   - 'drift':
     * For day < 35: drift = 0.0
     * For day >= 35: drift = -0.5
   - Theoretical impact on probabilities:
     * At z = 0.0 : P(accept) drops 50.0% -> 37.8%; P(mutual) drops 25.0% -> 14.3% (-43.0%)
     * At z = 0.5 : P(accept) drops 62.2% -> 50.0%; P(mutual) drops 38.7% -> 25.0% (-35.5%)
     * At z = -0.5: P(accept) drops 37.8% -> 26.9%; P(mutual) drops 14.3% -> 7.2%  (-49.3%)

5. WHY EACH VARIANT IS HARD & POLICY RECOMMENDATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) 'sparse':
   - Cause: 12 zones with 0 cross-zone willingness shrinks the graph density by 83%.
   - Hardship: Greedy baseline runs out of viable matches immediately; member coverage
     tanks to 14.5% (most members never receive an introduction).
   - Solution: Maximize intra-zone matching immediately; batch queries by zone; prioritize
     asking constraints for members residing in populated zones to create matchable pairs.

2) 'cold_start':
   - Cause: 70%+ hard constraint missingness creates an initial graph where 94% of potential
     edges are blocked by unknown constraints.
   - Hardship: With an ask budget of 12 units/day (each constraints bundle costs 3 = max 4
     members/day), greedy takes weeks to unlock the pool, leaving members idle and unserved.
   - Solution: Active learning / targeted constraint acquisition. Ask constraints for members
     who have the highest centrality or likelihood of pairing with already-feasible members.

3) 'delayed':
   - Cause: Date scheduling takes 6-26 days (mean 21.8 days). Max observed delay is 33 days.
   - Hardship: Dates occurring >30 days after intro are disqualified from MSMI. Also, members
     are locked in 'busy' state for `date_day + 6` days (up to 39 days).
   - Solution: Front-load introductions early in the 60-day horizon so dates occur well before
     the final follow-up window; keep track of busy schedules and plan allocations around delays.

4) 'shift':
   - Cause: Unobserved change in true utility weights.
     - `relationship_pace`: 0.8 (most important)
     - `conversations`: 0.5 (second most important)
     - `relationship_goal`: 0.25 (reduced from 0.70)
     - `lifestyle`: -0.25 (opposites attract; matching lifestyle penalizes acceptance!)
   - Hardship: Greedy weights all matches uniformly (+1) and actively pairs people with
     identical lifestyles, degrading match quality and acceptance probability.
   - Solution: Learn or detect preference weights from observed binary feedback (`introduction_response`),
     or calibrate soft matching scores using logistic regression / bandit feedback.

5) 'drift':
   - Cause: Systematic -0.5 logit drop in user responsiveness/acceptance starting at day 35.
   - Hardship: First-meeting mutual acceptance falls by up to 50% in the second half of the
     horizon. A policy saving candidates for later will suffer severe attrition.
   - Solution: Aggressively match viable candidates during days 0-34 when acceptance rates
     are highest. In the drift phase (days 35-59), only match high-confidence pairs with strong
     soft-feature alignment to overcome the -0.5 penalty.
