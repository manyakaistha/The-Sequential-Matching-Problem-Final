## Surrogate limitations retained in the frozen contract

D independently samples returners using one pooled online Beta rate, assumes
all currently available actors survive tomorrow, omits arrivals/new answers/
future feedback, and has zero tail beyond tomorrow. Public returners may share
an introduction; the independent Bernoulli approximation is not a calibrated
joint law. Even an unrelated potential return can enable evaluation of a deletion
whose predicted gain comes from changing day/tenure features. A deletion can
lose the other endpoint's opportunity. These are bounded heuristic assumptions,
not source deductions about expected opportunity cost or survival.

Soft evaluation uses the current hard-verified graph. It rescans the complete
current greedy/repair matching for each hypothetical answer to one candidate
question, under empirical currently disclosed categories. It examines at most
eight high-degree question actions and at most eight categories each. It does
not jointly simulate the retained hard-answer batch, selected soft answers,
selection-dependent missingness or answer declines. Several independently ranked
questions can have redundant value. Gains are floored at zero and zero-gain
questions may still fill slack. For D, question values use its immediate greedy
parent, not the deployed continuation objective. Thus this is an immediate
marginal package heuristic, not calibrated VOI or a D2 controller. The protocol
now discloses these approximations; query legality and budget remain exact.
