## Gaps this campaign addresses and limits

Address reward calibration and missingness; dependence/censoring and feedback timing; query-budget use; allocation conflicts; continuation opportunity costs and late-horizon behavior; public-only interfaces; runtime, provenance, paired-world and multiplicity integrity. It cannot exhaust all algorithms or prove that every possible observation-only policy has been tried. Weak prediction or a negative final result is evidence against this finite portfolio, not a universal impossibility theorem.

Detailed implementation interfaces, thresholds, development grid, seed review and independent review receipts must be frozen before the relevant execution stage. Later infrastructure-only amendments preserve raw identities and are explicitly versioned.
