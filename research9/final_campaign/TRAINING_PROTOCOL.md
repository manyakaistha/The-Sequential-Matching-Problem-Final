# Fixed training and selection details

Frozen before fitting: direct and three-stage conditional dyad logistic families only; L2=1 summed weighted loss, training-only standardization, no hyperparameter sweep. Terminal controls are training-only Jeffreys-smoothed constant and logistic intercept+slope on the seven-field agreement count. Fit weights normalize every seed/family/arm cell equally; conditional-stage fits inherit the unconditional row weights on their declared risk sets. Conditional stages with fewer than50positive or50negative training examples use a zero-feature constant logistic model, not extra data. Never multiply independent endpoint marginal probabilities.

Probability predictions are clipped identically to[1e-6,1-1e-6] for terminal evaluation. Calibration is the absolute difference of weighted mean prediction and outcome; fixed reliability boundaries0,.005,.01,.02,.05,1. Fit all features only from64training seed groups. Select direct versus funnel by minimum validation terminal log loss, direct on exact ties. Report both fixed search entries. Bootstrap seed losses2000times using fixed RNG9139. Prediction admission requires train≥50positive, validation/development-test≥20positive each; selected logloss below both controls,95%bootstrap upper loss difference versus constant below0, and absolute mean calibration error≤.01 on each evaluated held-out split. No replacement after inspecting development-test.

Released response counts/rates are public descriptive histories and may include responses released before an introduction's deadline. They are not asserted to be unbiased actor response propensities; training labels and conditional-stage outcomes use full follow-up. Missing questionnaire answers are distinct from disagreement; public declines count as declines even without an answer timestamp.

If probability admission fails, the exact same selected asset may support only the predeclared three exploratory ranking candidates. No calibrated-value or continuation claim is allowed; the waiting candidate is omitted. Final policy value is decided by a separately frozen fresh experiment, not by predictive loss.

Development screen uses the first8validation groups in split.json's fixed hash order, all six families, unchangedG/Q6/P and the finite candidate list. All phases are cold public-request invocations, with validated legal actions and full eventual reward. No fitting occurs during the screen. Exact original reference actions/metrics must reproduce for reused worlds. Select highest mean candidate reward with positive paired mean versusG; exact ties prefer model_greedy,model_soft,model_repair,model_continuation in that order. A negative own-parent contrast is reported, not concealed. Invalidity/resource/audit failure admits no candidate; no automatic fallback or silent cell rerun.

No policy qualifies if all candidate means fail to exceedG. Otherwise one immutable asset/policy specification is selected before final outcomes. The final96fresh groups are evaluated once with selectedC,G,Q6,P. Formal one-sided alpha.05 tests follow the registered sequenceC-G,C-Q6,C-P; later formal claims require every earlier rejection. Report two-sided95%intervals descriptively and all paired seed differences. Final reward, predictive or runtime failure is final; no repair or added seeds to obtain significance.

Continuation scope amendment before fitting/outcome selection: candidateD is an explicitly heuristic one-day whole-action surrogate, not an admitted joint return law or Bellman policy. It models only up to8public rejection-clock potential returners using an online pooledBeta rate and16fixed shared scenarios, assumes currently available members survive tomorrow, and forecasts no anonymous arrivals, new answers or unreleased feedback. It considers the parent plus deletion of one of five weak parent edges, at most one hold per actor and no hold at59. Terminal-q admission is required to include it but does not certify these transition assumptions. Constructed tests must expose its finite-horizon/survival/supply limitations. Its prospective policy test can establish reward value for the frozen heuristic; it cannot validate a universal continuation theorem. A full12-day/D2planner is outside this finite last campaign.

Soft acquisition is a bounded immediate marginal package heuristic: at most8eligible soft actions, empirical currently disclosed answer categories, whole deployed current matching rescoring per answer. It omits same-batch hard-answer effects, joint soft-answer dependence and answer-decline probabilities. All asks are still one nonadaptive batch; hard Q6 questions are preserved and only genuine slack is spent. Zero estimated marginal gains may fill slack; this is a ranking decision, not proof of positive information value.

Pre-fit edge cases: the constant control uses mean-one normalized fit weights with explicitJeffreys0.5/1smoothing; it is not an intercept refit. Empty conditional risk sets use a fixed0.5zero-trial prior; insufficient stage support otherwise uses its training-only smoothed constant. Failed terminal support counts disable probability/continuation admission but preserve the predeclared exploratory ranking path. The bootstrap upper endpoint is quantile.975 (upper endpoint of a two-sided95%percentile interval, conservatively stronger than a one-sided95%upper bound). Every NPZ, its complete declared grid/seed split and immutable extraction receipt/hash must be reverified before fitting.
