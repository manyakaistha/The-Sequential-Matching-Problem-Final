# Primary literature and applicability

Checked during planning on 11 October 2026. Literature motivates designs; it does not establish a simulator win. Existing verified citations remain in [docs/REFERENCES.md](../docs/REFERENCES.md). This file adds the closest mechanisms for the proposed next tests. Primary abstracts/proceedings and proposal-source papers were inspected; the complete proofs of every paper were not independently reproduced.

| Primary source | Inherited idea | Simulator adaptation and transfer limit |
|---|---|---|
| Delong, Farhadi, Niazadeh, Sivan, Udwani, [Online Bipartite Matching with Reusable Resources](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.07084) | Periodic reranking and correlated selection for reusable unit resources | Motivate N2 persistence across return cycles. Their one-sided bipartite setting and deterministic use duration differ from this general graph's two reusable endpoints and permanent successful pauses. No competitive ratio transfers. |
| Goyal, Iyengar, Udwani, [Online Allocation of Reusable Resources via Algorithms Guided by Fluid Approximations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.03983) | Fluid treatment of stochastic resource usage | Motivate occupancy kernels/relaxations and approximate continuation. Large-inventory guarantees do not apply to individual actors; their independent usage setup differs from shared dyadic outcomes/retirement. |
| Borodin, MacRury, Rakheja, [Prophet Matching Meets Probing with Commitment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.04325) | Stochastic probing constraints and LP benchmark design | Motivate N3's acquisition-versus-allocation formulation. Here node bundles reveal correlated incident legality without forcing an introduction; the paper's edge probing and commitment assumptions differ. |
| Bansal et al., [When LP Is the Cure for Your Matching Woes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.5356) | Stochastic matching and probing relaxations | A close formulation for uncertainty and matching feasibility; bundle disclosure/reuse require a new validity argument, not imported approximation factors. |
| Golovin, Krause, [Adaptive Submodularity](https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.3967) | Conditions supporting greedy information gathering | Check the required diminishing returns. Two-endpoint verification is complementary, so generic greedy information guarantees cannot simply be assumed. |
| Mandi, Bucarey, Mulamba, Guns, [Decision-Focused Learning: Through the Lens of Learning to Rank](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/mandi22a.html) | Train around quality of decisions, not generic prediction loss | Motivate L2/decision-margin backlog. Full alternative matching outcomes are not observed in deterministic logs; model-dependent targets and independent reward evaluation must be disclosed. |
| Wilder, Dilkina, Tambe, [Melding the Data-Decisions Pipeline](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/3982) | Learning through combinatorial decision objectives | Supports testing objective alignment. Continuous relaxations do not solve sequential occupancy or identify unchosen outcomes in this simulator. |
| Russo, Van Roy, [Learning to Optimize via Information-Directed Sampling](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2014/hash/90720a2fcc41f9332e6a1558da327089-Abstract.html) | Balance regret against information about valuable actions | Motivate a gated exploration backlog. Information must affect later legal opportunities before horizon/retirement; coupled matchings and delays need their own analysis. |
| Dudík, Langford, Li, [Doubly Robust Policy Evaluation and Learning](https://icml.cc/2011/papers/554_icmlpaper.pdf) | Combine reward and behavior-policy models in supported contextual actions | Deterministic previous matching logs lack overlap for most new actions; doubly robust estimation is not a cure for zero support or sequential occupancy confounding. |
| Jiang, Li, [Doubly Robust Off-policy Value Evaluation for Reinforcement Learning](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/jiang16.html) | Sequential off-policy evaluation | More appropriate than pretending every edge is an independent bandit, but action support and likelihood-ratio variance remain requirements. Direct paired policy evaluation remains final arbiter. |

Dynamic waiting, odd-set matching polytopes, calibrated probabilities, shrinkage, delayed-feedback learning, Blackwell information ordering and multiplicity are established topics in the preserved bibliography. None is newly discovered here. The new work could contribute a simulator-specific joint survivor likelihood, publicly conditioned return model, certified renewal bound, complementary batch objective, exact counterexample benchmark or a verified policy improvement. Those contributions must be demonstrated before calling them novel; broad novelty remains uncertain.

For each promising candidate's final report, state: closest method; inherited components; precise new observation/model/decision result; conditions needed beyond this simulator; known counterexamples; and independently verified performance. A new implementation in this repository alone is not algorithmic novelty. No claim is made about real-world reciprocal matching outcomes.
