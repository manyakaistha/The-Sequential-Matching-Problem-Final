## Finding

The shipped transition/observation rules support legal collision-aware hard acquisition, bounded allocation repair, return distributions, and public-history models. They do **not** supply future release/exit times or independent PRNG-stream proofs. `research6/PLAN.md` section 3 and `STRUCTURAL_PROPOSALS.md:24–51,145–160` correctly distinguish these scopes. The round-five recorder and exhaustive replay audit provide useful architecture, but copying the runner unchanged would fail several R6 logging, interruption, isolation, and provenance requirements.

## Contradictions and implementation risks requiring explicit resolution

1. **Fallback mismatch:** `INTERFACE.md` as read requires an empty legal fallback; `STRUCTURAL_PROPOSALS.md:506–508` requires reconstruction of shipped greedy. Both are legal. They have different action/value semantics, must be documented before configuration freeze, and must be identical across contribution controls. Any emergency fallback is counted and affects eligibility. G must always invoke the shipped functions unchanged, not silently inherit a generic empty fallback.
2. **Names:** registry ID P is the frozen population/FIFO control, while structural Algorithm P is component lookahead and corresponds to N6. Algorithm R supplies part of N5/N3 calendar work. Algorithm I maps to N4 and D to L2. Store registry IDs plus mechanism names to prevent control/planner confusion.
3. **Interface diagnostics:** `INTERFACE.md` returns exactly actions+memory; `TEST_SPEC.md:60,76` requires candidate choices, predictions, stopping reason and RNG provenance. Establish a host-only diagnostic channel or registered public-memory envelope. Do not add unvalidated response keys, read host files, or make stdout non-JSON to satisfy logging.
4. **Legal asks versus protocol:** `Simulator.resolve_asks` can be called repeatedly within remaining budget, but `evaluate.episode` exposes one ask phase. Simulator-level convenience tests must not admit an adaptive two-batch evaluated policy.
5. **Exact date versus release:** a received date gives D+6 busy boundary, with survival/pause uncertainty. A guessed lag or hidden `busy` value is not an observation. Public return forecasting must condition on missing/pending feedback and observed availability.
6. **Same seed versus pairing:** scenario families share a seed cluster, but their generation branches differ and their worlds need not share member identities. World hashes must match across policies within each seed/family; do not require identical hashes across families or treat assignments as independent observations.
7. **No source theorem transfers:** local matching improvement, cardinality improvement, better Brier/log loss, accurate return forecasts, exact tiny DP, and full-truth greedy scores are not proofs of full sequential policy gain. Treat snapshot headroom and privileged ceilings separately from causal policy reward.

This proposal deliberately does not read prior confirmation for fitting/admission. `research4/EXPERIMENT_REGISTRY.md:36,54,62` identifies B seeds80001–80012 as exploratory screen, D82001–82020 as selection, and E83001 onward as confirmation. Existing paths include `results/research4/screen_traces/greedy_<family>_<seed>.json` and `results/research4/smoke_traces/greedy_development_101.json`; fingerprint exact files/manifest/archive indexes before use.

- Wiring: seed101 smoke requests/trajectories, all six families only if their examined provenance is verified. This is protocol checking, not independent reward evidence.
- Primary inventory: greedy round-four B seeds80001–80004 × six families =24 examined episodes, all60 pre/post-ask days. Deterministic smallest-four rule avoids favorable trajectory selection. Record the entire twelve-seed B scope as the broader available source; do not label the subset exhaustive for B. Reconstruct observations from saved actions/world only after authorization and call no policy in the inventory path.
- Graph/query inventory: all1440 action-day states, before/after asks; eligible/unverified/declined counts, legal unused edges, degree/components, greedy cardinality/local-exchange gaps, query singleton/pair complementarity and neighborhood collisions, verification delays. Source-state reconstruction distinguishes final disclosed values from assignment-time values.
- Forecast/scoring diagnostic: fixed action days0,10,20,30,40,50,59 on that cohort (168 days per phase), recording prospective public beliefs and privileged labels in separate tables. Freeze policy-owned samples/configuration; do not retain whichever sample salt predicts examined labels best.
- Resource ceilings are separate tasks: 300CPU seconds for basic graph/funnel/history inventory and a proposed1800CPU seconds for sampled batch/return/oracle diagnostics. Before broader A, benchmark the diagnostic work on wiring states under separate authorization.

All tables report pooled numerators/denominators **and** seed/family-balanced episode summaries. Precedence for endpoint-days is arrival→hard decline→exit→observed positive pause→busy→unverified→no legal unused neighbor→legal unassigned; hard-infeasible/used-dyad reasons belong in explicit pair-level opportunity partitions to avoid double counting an actor with several differently excluded partners.
