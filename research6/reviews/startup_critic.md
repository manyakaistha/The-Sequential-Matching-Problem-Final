## Strongest objections to any eventual positive narrative

- **Searched noise:** at B40 and old SD .2314, one contrast SE is about .0366, larger than the +.025 admission gate. Breadth can nominate lucky mechanisms even with honest fresh E. C sign checks and D90% protect compute imperfectly; they do not confer confirmatory causal credit. Report every tested line and preserve no-contender termination.
- **No usable horizon/headroom:** thin verified graphs, absent return alternatives and scarce resolved second-stage histories can make sophisticated models irrelevant. More accurate posterior probabilities or stronger current matchings need not increase sequential qualified reward. Privileged greedy/controller nulls are not global bounds; exact toy wins are not full-simulator wins.
- **Dependence and survivor bias:** shared actor rates/dyad effects, selected stage existence and successful-actor permanent pauses undermine independent marginal multiplication and naive repeat-history fitting. Pending labels are not negatives. Missing-profile sampling must reuse a member realization across all incident edges; sampled feasibility never makes a real edge legal.
- **Lucky deadlines/action streams:** raw qualified reward remains primary. A changed dyad/day changes downstream keyed randomness. A .36 pause identity is an expectation under specified independent-stream assumptions, not an automatic conditional Rao–Blackwell estimator for adaptive calendar/global-learning policies. Surrogate success cannot rescue failed raw confirmation.
- **Variance/power optimism:** D selects the largest lower bound, which can favor both high estimated mean and low estimated SD. The SD floor/inflation is a heuristic, not a bound or simulator 90% power certificate. Neither Bonferroni nor bootstrapping fixes leakage, adaptive sample extension, cell omissions or mismatched parents.
- **Packaging success is not scientific success:** perfect archives, replay and production establish recorded behavior within their tested scope; they cannot turn a zero-crossing interval into an improvement or prove greedy optimality.
