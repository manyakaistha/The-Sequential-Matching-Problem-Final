## 1. Governance Protocol and Scientific Ledger Principles

This ledger records every major empirical claim, hypothesis test, confirmatory trial, and causal interpretation advanced across the lifecycle of the Sequential Matching Problem research program.

Each entry adheres to strict forensic standards:
1. **Immutable Traceability**: Explicitly linked to frozen source code, machine-readable JSON/CSV logs, and cryptographic hashes.
2. **Cluster-Level Statistical Unit**: Differentiates true independent randomization units (seed clusters) from repeated intra-seed scenario rows.
3. **Explicit Multiplicity Accounting**: Distinguishes unadjusted nominal $p$-values from FWER-controlled step-down Holm-Bonferroni corrections.
4. **Mandatory Limitation and Correction Ledger**: Documents identified statistical flaws (e.g., Independent Observation Fallacy, feedback horizon errors, snapshot censoring) and records their definitive corrections.

## 3. Methodological Ledger of Scientific Fallacies and Corrective Rules

### 3.1 The Independent Observation Fallacy
- **The Error**: In early Round 2 analysis (`research9/round2/analyze.py`), the 16 scenario variations evaluated on each seed were pooled as if they were 16 independent random draws, asserting $N = 192$ degrees of freedom and yielding a spuriously tight confidence interval [$+0.040, +0.158$] and $p = 0.0009$ for $G_{\text{arr}}$.
- **The Correction**: Scenario laws applied to the same seed population share the identical underlying member traits and arrivals. The proper unit of independent replication is the **seed cluster** ($S = 12$). Aggregating contrasts within seed yields the honest interval [$-0.0701, +0.2680$] with Holm $p = 0.8965$.

### 3.2 The Day-60 Feedback Cutoff Fallacy
- **The Error**: Explaining the apparent advantage of arrival-ordered questioning by asserting that asking earlier arrivals allows their feedback to arrive before day 60.
- **The Correction**: The simulator enforces an explicit 40-day follow-up horizon ($t = 60$ to $99$). All introductions made up to day 59 are permitted their full 30-day date window and 3-day feedback windows. No outcome is lost to a day-60 barrier.

### 3.3 The False Equivalence Fallacy (Prior Invariance)
- **The Error**: Concluding that "matching is invariant to prior beliefs" because simulator-informed, empirical, and flat priors produced non-significant outcome differences on 12 seeds.
- **The Correction**: Statistical failure to reject the null hypothesis on a sample of 12 clusters is not proof of equivalence ($H_0: \Delta = 0$). Furthermore, empirical priors produced substantially worse calibration and severe date underprediction due to snapshot censoring ($0.4762$ vs $0.78$).
