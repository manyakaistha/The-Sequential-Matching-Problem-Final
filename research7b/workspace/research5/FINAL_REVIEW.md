## Scientific conclusion

The frozen population-ranking policy has **not defeated greedy**. All 430 registered seed clusters and all six families are present: 5,160 completed episodes, with neither committed failures nor committed fallback increments. Independently recomputed paired scenario-balanced effect is +0.0007751938 MSMI/100. The registered 98.75% interval is [-0.0272154463, +0.0287658339]; nominal two-sided p is 0.9446508327. Neither superiority nor inferiority is established. The adjusted upper endpoint excludes the targeted +0.05 effect for this frozen policy and simulator mixture under the registered inference assumptions. This is not a registered equivalence result, proof of greedy optimality, or rejection of every possible predictive policy.

Population qualified count is 1,972 versus greedy 1,968. Each policy has 2,580 episodes and 516,000 arrived members. The estimand averages episode MSMI/100 within each seed over six equally weighted families, then averages seeds; it is not an assignment-independent test. Because all episode denominators are 200 here, a pooled-count mean coincides numerically, but the seed-cluster uncertainty calculation remains essential. The public `missing_feedback` metric counts explicit no-response events; it must not be described as pending-outcome censoring. Full 100-day follow-up is retained.

The director's analysis and the independent statistician's saved calculations agree. This critic independently recomputed the complete grid and primary statistics from committed rows rather than accepting a reported confidence level. No claim of improved prediction is supported by this policy-reward test alone.

## Interrupted attempts are a material limitation

Nine uncommitted public invocation logs are retained across the two interruption archives. Their 488 parsable successful responses all match the final action at the same day and phase. Three complete uncommitted traces also exactly match final actions, introductions and completed public feedback. This is strong evidence against selecting among different realized outcomes for those retained traces; it does not reconstruct lost bytes or unknown pending invocations.

One archived match request explicitly records `KeyboardInterrupt` in the normal eligibility computation. It is compatible with external interruption, rather than evidence of an algorithmic numerical error, but it is still a recorded unsuccessful request. Three orphan gzip logs have CRC or missing-end errors. One corrupted log contains duplicate sequence 10; duplicates are not independent trials. Remaining unfinished requests and unwritten statuses cannot be declared successful. Archive hashes can preserve already damaged files without proving that they decompress successfully.

Therefore the defensible claim is **zero failures and fallbacks in the 5,160 committed completed episodes**. A claim of zero failures across every attempted invocation would be false or unsupported. The registered completed-grid eligibility gate is satisfied on its stated records; interrupted attempts are disclosed separately and not treated as failed completed episodes discarded in favor of new outcomes. If that gate were interpreted as requiring every attempted request to be successful, these interruption records prevent asserting it. The negative scientific conclusion does not depend on suppressing this distinction.

The critic checked archive manifest paths and byte sizes for all 747 listed records and hashed the nine orphan logs; it did not independently hash every archived world or inspect privileged worlds. Full archive sealing is a separate provenance responsibility. Policy inputs in reviewed code are public JSON observations, not the separately stored initial worlds, final hidden state, seed labels or scenario labels. No such prohibited input path was identified.

## Decision and unresolved objections

Reject a superiority or practically meaningful improvement claim for the frozen population control. Preserve it as a legal, reproducible negative result with a tighter effect interval than the earlier exploratory/confirmation point gain. Retain greedy as the supported baseline rather than promoting a searched point estimate as a winner.

No material numerical or policy-input correctness blocker was found for the stated completed fixed-grid comparison. Required reporting limitations are the finite multiplicity family, parametric cluster-interval assumptions and bootstrap sensitivity, operational recovery with unknown interrupted requests, tested production scope, and the distinction between source consistency and historical provenance. Package/archive completion remains a separate obligation, not evidence already established by this review. No mathematical optimality or universal attainable-headroom theorem follows from these empirical findings.
