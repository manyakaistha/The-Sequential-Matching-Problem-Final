## Evidence categories and limits

1. **Proven mathematical results:** no new theorem in this verification round. Preserved round-four response-factorization and deadline-invariance results retain their stated scopes.
2. **Exact simulator calculations:** committed action histories, public qualification rules, counts and source-linked initial/final states are checked as recorded synthetic simulator computations.
3. **Exact simplified-model solutions:** none added; inherited small-model benchmarks are preserved, not presented as full-simulator optimality results.
4. **Empirical findings:** the fixed430-seed effect and uncertainty above; production and replay equality within the stated tested scope.
5. **Approximation assumptions:** paired t inference relies on independent seed clusters and normal/large-sample approximation; finite four-test alpha allocation is not an anytime guarantee. Six families sharing a seed are treated as correlated. Bootstrap is sensitivity only. Source hashes prove current byte consistency, not tamper-proof historical timestamps or unknown external seed usage.
6. **Unvalidated hypotheses:** future ranking, learning or dynamic improvements remain open. This package does not nominate another searched candidate or extend the test until significance.
