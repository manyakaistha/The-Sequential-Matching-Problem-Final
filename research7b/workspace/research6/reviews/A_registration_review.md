This script does not measure most of those. An absence of a current K gap cannot reject query, waiting, rollout or learning hypotheses. ## Overall gate and strongest remaining objection

Proceed with the amended, frozen public audit as an opportunity measurement subtask. Defer fresh reward grids until inventory closure and actual candidate-specific A/source/resource/production/review gates pass. A result can legitimately reject/defer a fixed allocation candidate, but cannot show that the entire research objective is impossible or that unimplemented mechanisms have failed. Conversely, a current gap plus 1% action change is far below evidence of a +.05 sequential gain: extra introductions can occupy future alternatives, changed dyad/day streams add noise, and nuisance shipped scores need not reward good edge choices.

The bounded deterministic input selection and explicit negative stopping are sound. Preserve their scope, every failure and deferred line, then let complete parent contrasts and untouched confirmation answer the scientific question.
