## Offline: strongest case and strongest opposition

Developer's strongest case: a compact regularized observed-feature score trained
on old seed-separated assignments discriminates without fitting sparse actor
histories. Fresh B gain is distributed across seeds rather than only one extreme
world, and it produces160positive pauses versus137greedy. Runtime and protocol
remain simple and legal. Frozen coefficients were embedded before B.

Critic's strongest case: only26 training positives produced the slopes. Old
held-out prediction was worse than public population integration on identical
examples. Fitted lifestyle and conversation slopes are negative, and that cannot
be interpreted as a discovered preference law. Its sigmoid is monotone and all
scores positive: common intercept and common day≥35 term have no effect on
within-invocation ranking or chosen pairs. The actual allocation is a fixed
linear equality-feature ordering, not demonstrated adaptive late-horizon control.
Any calibration advantage of those common terms cannot explain pair choice.

Observed B: offline has5236assignments versus5299baseline,700mutual accepts
versus718,571dates versus572, but68qualified versus51. Positive pauses160versus137
and deadline-disqualified positives92versus86. Thus assignment count, acceptance
or dates do not explain terminal gain monotonically. Candidate selection,
later-stage preference effects and favorable realized qualification draws are
alternative explanations; chosen-trajectory counts do not isolate them.

Falsifier/registered test: C reward replication with frozen coefficients, compared
also with clean, population and random_order. Score all models on the same C
baseline assignment-time cohort to check prediction, with seed-level uncertainty.
A reward loss in C cannot be rescued by B p-values or predictive point scores.
If it tracks random_order, a distinctive benefit of learned coefficients remains
unestablished. No coefficient fitting or feature changes after B.

## Goal queries: strongest case and strongest opposition

Developer's strongest case: goal equality improves both reciprocal acceptance and
second-stage logits in every family. The policy uses only otherwise-unused daily
budget, retains FIFO hard bundles, asks current verified edge endpoints and can
change pair rankings using immediately observed answers. This is a legitimate
acquisition intervention under the JSON protocol.

Critic's strongest case: versus greedy it also changes scoring weights and
missingness treatment. The correct acquisition effect compares goal_queries to
its own public_weight parent, not all of +.097222 versus greedy. In B that parent
scores .409722; the query difference is only+.041667. Query policy has4945
introductions versus4963parent,740mutual versus748,592dates versus600,171pauses
versus156, and65qualified versus59. More information does not necessarily mean
more early funnel events. It can alter occupancy and future choices, while
favorable second/deadline outcomes on a few dyad/day streams can mimic value.

Falsifier/registered test: C contains both policies. Analyze their paired
seed-cluster reward contrast and independently compare query answers that change
scores/chosen pairs. Report acquisition cost, positive pauses, timing/occupancy,
repeat endpoints and deadlines; classify trajectory q sums as descriptive.
A positive contrast to greedy with nonpositive contrast to public_weight does not
establish acquisition value. The parent comparison belongs to a predeclared
exploratory ablation family with multiplicity accounting, not an additional
unplanned confirmatory claim.

## Random order: strongest case and strongest opposition

Developer's strongest case: feature ranking may not earn complexity in a sparse,
mostly missing market. A deterministic public ID/day hash avoids a common
canonical ordering and changes competing dyad choices without model fitting,
queries or hidden inputs. Fresh B estimate is comparable to more elaborate methods.

Critic's strongest case: the method predicts no preference or continuation value;
its positive searched estimate may simply be favorable hash-order realization.
Only one hash rule was tested. It is not evidence all random tie-breaking is
superior, that random allocation is optimal, or that informative scores never
matter. Coverage4826served versus4834greedy and5243introductions versus5299 do not
support a blanket increased-cardinality mechanism. It has155pauses and64qualified,
versus137/51greedy, but these realized counts do not show why the changed choices
succeeded. Pair/day outcome streams change with the choices.

Falsifier/registered test: C repeats the exact hash rule on fresh IDs/seeds.
Compare to clean/population/offline as registered. No selecting a new salt or
hash function after outcomes. If gains regress, retain the negative result;
if they persist, confirmation tests that specific frozen rule, not all randomness.

## Disappointing controls and structural caution

Observed: clean scores .340278, below greedy; removing three directly unused
soft fields did not establish a reward gain. That does not refute their direct
conditional irrelevance: changed matching order/cardinality/future states still
matter. Canonical .333333 also underperforms on this screen. Scarce .423611 has
no adjusted superiority and the exact scarce counterexample remains relevant.

Observed: population and throughput have identical byte action digests on all72
B worlds, not merely equal means. Throughput adds a computation without an
observed action change in this tested domain. This provides no evidence of a
busy-time policy mechanism; it is not proof their rankings coincide universally.
Population +.006944 does not vindicate better predictor → better reward.
Four_queries has no demonstrated advantage earning its extra4996ask units versus
baseline; its queried parent and C's focused acquisition contrast remain distinct.

## Decision and unresolved objections

Approve C as the registered exploratory replication, with all seven prelisted
policies and32complete seed clusters. Do not retune or add a new candidate from
this review. C/D independent seed separation and frozen E remain essential.

exist; it will assess implementation reproducibility, not increase independent
statistical sample size. Candidate-specific new-round confirmation is required
before proposing deployment. None of these B candidates is presently statistically
superior under the searched-family criterion.
